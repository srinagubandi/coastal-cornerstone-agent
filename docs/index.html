MCP endpoint

URL: https://coastal-cornerstone-agent-production.up.railway.app/mcp

Paste this public HTTPS URL into any client that speaks the 2025-03-26 tools subset. No authentication is required for the two public tools: get_faq (optional FAQ ID) and ask_company_question (question only).

import asyncio
from mcp import Client

async def main():
    async with Client("https://coastal-cornerstone-agent-production.up.railway.app/mcp") as client:
        result = await client.call_tool("get_faq", {})
        print(result.structured_content)

asyncio.run(main())

General guidance is not a company promise or project engineering/safety/legal advice. Non-exact wording can be matched, but satisfaction and relevance are not guaranteed. Unknown pricing, dates, insurance, warranties, availability, founding, or similar details are handled through the published company website contact form, not referred directly to Ben. review_queued means bounded private retention only – no email, follow-up, action, or answer is promised.

Protective limits

Questions: 5/minute and 50/UTC day per client hash; 60/minute and 1,000/UTC day globally. Public reads: 120/minute per client and 1,200/minute globally. HTTP requests are additionally bounded by transport, body, and connection limits.

Exact question/tag matches avoid paid model calls. Non-exact selection uses a pinned model with live price checks and a $1 UTC-month cap. At most three paid requests may run concurrently.

Pricing, budget, or knowledge unavailability produces a fallback – never an invented answer.

MCP tools subset

The /mcp endpoint speaks JSON-RPC 2.0 and supports initialize (version 2025-03-26), notifications/initialized, tools/list, and tools/call. The tools are get_faq and ask_company_question. Requests share the same API protections. Notifications receive HTTP 204.

This is not a full Streamable HTTP, SSE, or A2A server. No batch JSON-RPC, remote tools, arbitrary URLs, CRM access, booking, quoting, emailing, or engineering advice are supported.

Grounding and privacy

For business facts, the model selects closed-set evidence IDs. For general construction queries, it may return a validated bounded high-level general explanation explicitly labeled General construction guidance. Questions never become knowledge automatically.

API/MCP questions are logged privately after email, phone, URL, IP, and credential redaction. Salted IP hashes enforce rate limits without storing raw IPs. This offers limited pseudonymity, not guaranteed anonymity; names and addresses may remain. Do not submit personal or confidential data. The service cannot authenticate an AI caller’s identity without a caller credential.

Private events are retained 30 days (10,000-event cap). Deduplicated review questions have a 30-day lifetime, a 100-new-question/UTC-day cap, and a 1,000-pending cap. Email digest delivery is paused by default and requires owner-approved configuration; no empty digest is sent.
